The Art of Lost-Wax Goldsmithing

Every AURUM piece begins not on a computer screen, but in pure hand-carved jeweler's wax.

Our master goldsmiths shape organic contours, testing weight and ergonomic balance against the human form. When molten gold at 1,064°C replaces the wax, a seamless sculptural jewel emerges—free of soldering seams and imbued with immortal warmth.
