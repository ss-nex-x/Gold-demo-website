FAQ

Common questions

What is gold purity testing?

It is a measurement of how much gold an item actually contains, expressed as a karat value or a percentage. It tells you what your piece is made of rather than what it looks like.

Can old or inherited jewellery be tested?

Yes. Age, wear and design do not prevent testing. Bring the piece in and we will confirm what the test can determine for it.

Can silver be tested too?

Yes — silver jewellery and articles can be tested alongside gold items.
